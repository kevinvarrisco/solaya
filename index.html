Uluwatu · Bali · Indonesia

Where Nature Meets Timeless Luxury.

Set along the southern coast of Bali, Solaya Uluwatu is envisioned as a private sanctuary shaped by natural materials, thoughtful architecture, and the quiet beauty of its surroundings.

Here, high cliffs drop into turquoise surf, monolithic limestone structures open to gentle sea winds, and each sunrise invites an unhurried cadence of living tailored exclusively to a discerning few.

The Peninsula

Above the Southern Coast of Bali.

Perched on the southern tip of the Bukit Peninsula, Uluwatu is renowned for dramatic ocean-carved limestone bluffs, world-class surf breaks, and ancient temple rituals.

Solaya resides in complete seclusion along Jl. Pantai Suluban, offering absolute privacy while remaining intimately connected to the island's finest coastal spots.
